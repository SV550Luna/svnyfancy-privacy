Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026

This policy covers SocialForge Studio, a personal tool used by one person, the owner of the eBay store SvNyFancy, to publish her own product listings to her own social media accounts.

What the application uses

  • Photos, titles and descriptions of the owner's own eBay listings.
  • Access tokens for the owner's own social media accounts, stored locally on the owner's computer.

What is collected from other people

Nothing. The application has no other users, no visitors and no accounts. It does not collect personal data, does not use analytics and does not use cookies.

Sharing

No data is shared with third parties, sold, or used for advertising.

Storage

The application runs locally on the owner's computer. No servers are used and no data is stored elsewhere.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page.
